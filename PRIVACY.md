# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-21_

Monash Moodle Downloader is an unofficial browser extension. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Monash University.

## What the extension accesses

- **Monash Moodle pages.** When you click **Scan**, the extension reads the current page on `learning.monash.edu` and the Moodle pages it links to (resources, folders, assignments) to find course files, their names, types and sizes. It runs on no other website.
- **Your Moodle login.** Requests to Moodle use the session you are already signed in with. The extension never sees or stores your password.

## What the extension stores

- Your settings (file size limit, excluded file types, interface language) are stored locally in your browser with `chrome.storage` and `localStorage`.
- Downloaded files are saved by Chrome to your Downloads folder.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party.
- It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
- It does not sell, share or transfer user data, and does not use it for any purpose other than downloading the course files you select.
